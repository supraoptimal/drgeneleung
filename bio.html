Bio

Gene studied medicine and trained as an orthopaedic surgeon in Hong Kong, attaining fellowships locally and through Edinburgh. He later completed an LLB with the University of London. Alongside his practice, he explores the intersection of medicine, technology, systems, responsibility, and human judgment.

Education

  • MBChB The Chinese University of Hong Kong
  • MSc in Sports Medicine & Health Sciences The Chinese University of Hong Kong
  • ChM in Trauma & Orthopaedic Surgery, with Distinction The University of Edinburgh
  • LLB University of London

Professional

  • FHKCOS Fellowship of the Hong Kong College of Orthopaedic Surgeons
  • FHKAM Fellowship of the Hong Kong Academy of Medicine
  • FRCSEd Fellowship of the Royal College of Surgeons of Edinburgh
  • FRCS (Eng) Fellowship of the Royal College of Surgeons of England
